---
name: Bank bulk bundle pricing
description: Fixed-price Bank bulk bundles and their checkout constraints.
---

Bank bulk bundles must contain exactly 20 unique public bank-routing records and cost a fixed $1 per record ($20 total), independent of each record's stored individual price or any rank discount.

**Why:** The bulk price is a user-facing offer, and client-supplied quantities or totals could otherwise create underpriced, duplicate, or partial inventory sales.

**How to apply:** Treat the Bank bulk offer as a fixed-price product rule, separate from normal individual Bank pricing and rank incentives.
