---
name: Refund-only ticket resolution
description: Support and order resolution policy after retiring replacements.
---

Support ticket resolution is refund-only: users submit refund requests, and administrators may refund or resolve a ticket without refunding. Replacement must not be offered in active order or ticket workflows.

**Why:** The project owner explicitly retired the replacement system in favor of a clear refund-or-resolve process.

**How to apply:** Keep historical replacement records readable for audit/history, but do not add replacement buttons, routes, or ticket actions. New support requests should use the refund subject automatically.
